# Writing guidance

- This repository is the RankAI AI Workbook, intended for internal RankAI sharing.
- Collaborate with the author in Chinese. Polish their meaning without inventing experiences, results, or endorsements.
- English is the primary publication language and source of truth. Chinese is a companion translation. Keep repository metadata, PRs, and templates in English.
- Keep Chinese and English articles aligned under docs/zh-CN/ and docs/en/, using matching filenames.
- Use Markdown by default and Mermaid for useful process or decision diagrams. Store other visual assets under assets/.
- Clearly label drafts, assumptions, and points awaiting confirmation.
- Keep content specific to RankAI. Exclude unrelated employers, private account details, credentials, and confidential source material.
- notes/ is an ignored working area for raw material; do not publish it by default.
- Work locally until the user requests publication. Future hosting should use a private RankaiHQ repository with appropriate member access.
